Point32Health is hiring a Director of Research Computing, Data & Technology to develop and execute the Institute's research technology strategy and oversee research computing, research technology, and data enablement.

<!-- truncate -->

The Director of Research Computing, Data & Technology is responsible for developing and executing the Institute's research technology strategy and oversight of research computing, research technology, data enablement, and related support staff. The role combines strategic planning, innovation leadership, stakeholder engagement, governance, and program management with a deep understanding of research operations and scientific workflows.

The Director serves as the primary liaison among researchers, operational leadership, enterprise IT, compliance, and external partners to ensure that technology investments align with scientific priorities and institutional objectives.

The position directly supervises a team of four and is expected to provide leadership and oversight for research technology initiatives, including evaluating and, where warranted, redesigning the roles, structure, and responsibilities of this team to align with the Institute's research technology strategy.
