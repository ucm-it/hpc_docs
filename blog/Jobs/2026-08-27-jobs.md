I'm writing to share a job opportunity at the University of Illinois Chicago. UIC's Advanced Cyberinfrastructure for Education and Research (ACER) is hiring a Research Cloud Engineer to support computational and data-intensive research across the university.

The successful candidate will collaborate with UIC researchers to design, build, and maintain secure, scalable cloud and hybrid computing infrastructure. Key responsibilities include providing architectural expertise, developing cost-effective cloud solutions, and delivering technical training to researchers and staff.

For full details or to apply, check out the [UIC Job Posting](https://uic.csod.com/ux/ats/careersite/1/home/requisition/20743?c=uic).

Please feel free to forward this opportunity to anyone in your network who might be a good fit. Don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions!
