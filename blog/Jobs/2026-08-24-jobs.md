Columbia University is looking for a talented Sr. Research Systems Engineer (Secure Data & Research Computing) to join our Research Computing Services team in New York City.

If you or anyone you know might be interested, this position involves:

- **Secure Data Enclaves:** Administering Columbia's on-premise Secure Data Enclave (SDE) and maintaining compliance frameworks (HIPAA, CMMC, NIST 800-53/171).
- **Data Transfer:** Managing Columbia's Globus High Assurance Subscription as the primary technical point of contact.
- **Research Computing Consulting**
- **Community Outreach**

For full details or to apply, check out the [Columbia University Job Posting](https://opportunities.columbia.edu/jobs/sr-research-systems-engineer-secure-data-research-computing-manhattanville-new-york-united-states).

Feel free to share this opening with anyone in your network who might be a great fit!
