Virginia Tech's Advanced Research Computing team is hiring a Research Software Engineer, AI/ML in Blacksburg, Virginia.

This hands-on role supports AI/ML software and services for researchers, with opportunities to work on LLM infrastructure, GPU/HPC deployments, AI-driven applications, and researcher training. The position is eligible for some hybrid work but primarily on-site, with a competitive salary.

Applicants must have a master's degree or equivalent experience, strong Linux and software/web-service development experience, and experience with containerization. U.S. citizenship or permanent residency is required; sponsorship is not available.

Full details and the application can be found [here](https://jobs.apply.vt.edu/jobs/research-software-engineer-ai-ml-blacksburg-virginia-united-states-hybrid-062cca03-c39a-4fad-95ab-58440a122bb6).
