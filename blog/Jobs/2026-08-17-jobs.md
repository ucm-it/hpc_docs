The [Center for Advanced Computing (CAC) at Cornell University](https://www.cac.cornell.edu/default.aspx) seeks an experienced IT professional to join its leadership team as Associate Director for Consulting. Well-qualified candidates will be expert project managers who understand research computing environments and are equally adept at optimizing existing project workloads and bringing in new consulting projects.

You can find full details about the position and qualifications via the online posting on Cornell Careers ([https://jobs.hr.cornell.edu/us/en/job/WDR-00060631](https://jobs.hr.cornell.edu/us/en/job/WDR-00060631)). Review of applications will begin immediately.
