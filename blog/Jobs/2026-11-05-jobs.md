University of Wisconsin-Madison is hiring for a new research facilitator position to support their regulated research efforts!

Help shape the future of secure, impactful research at UW–Madison. As the Regulated Research Facilitator, you'll partner with researchers, IT, and compliance experts to navigate complex regulatory requirements, enable cutting-edge research, and remove barriers that stand between great ideas and discovery. Based in Madison, Wisconsin.

Applications are due Nov. 11, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. CST. See the [job posting](https://jobs.wisc.edu/jobs/regulated-research-facilitator-madison-wisconsin-united-states) for full details and to apply.
