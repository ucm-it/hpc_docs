University of Kentucky is hiring a tenure-track Research Data Analysis Librarian. Serving all academic disciplines, the Research Data Analysis Librarian supports robust, reproducible, and impactful research across campus by providing expertise in data analysis methods and workflows, while also engaging with emerging approaches, including the responsible use of artificial intelligence in research and scholarship.

Candidates should hold either 1) a Master's degree in Library and Information Science from an ALA-accredited program by start date or 2) a relevant, advanced degree. This is a great opportunity for anyone skilled in communicating technical concepts and workflows to researchers at all levels.

The full job posting can be found [here](https://ukjobs.uky.edu/postings/643224).

Review of applications will begin September 14. Please feel free to reach out with any questions about the position or working at University of Kentucky.
