Montana State University is hiring a System Administrator for Quantum and Research Computing, combining HPC system administration with facilitation and quantum computing expertise.

<!-- truncate -->

This is an exciting and unique opportunity in a positive and dynamic team at a growing R1 University in Bozeman, MT, one of the most desirable college towns in the US. This new position combines HPC system administration with facilitation and quantum computing expertise in support of Montana State University's innovative quantum computing initiatives, working closely with the Research Cyberinfrastructure and QCORE teams. The individual in this role will have the opportunity to build and advance MSU's quantum simulators and integrations between three on-site quantum computers and their HPC cluster while promoting and enabling novel uses for quantum computing, hybrid quantum-classical resources, and quantum simulation.

**System Administrator for Quantum and Research Computing**
Montana State University — University Information Technology and Physics

For the complete job announcement and application procedures, see the [job posting](https://jobs.montana.edu/postings/53710).

Screening of applications will begin on December 3, 2026; however, applications will continue to be accepted until an adequate applicant pool has been established.
