Davidson College is looking to grow its Research Computing team again. Specifically, they are looking to hire a dedicated Research Computing Facilitator.

Find more information at the [official posting](https://fa-exci-saasfaprod1.fa.ocs.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_2/job/942/).

They might not be big, but they are an RCU and while they do have a focus on undergrad education, they are ambitious. Since January, they've seen over 800 unique users touch one of their clusters, either for research and exploration or as part of a course.

This position is intended to support research and exploration efforts by faculty, staff, and students. That includes helping students make the jump from coursework to independent research, providing insights which help guide architectural decisions, and partnering with faculty as they navigate the research computing landscape at the campus, regional, and national levels.

If you know a researcher looking to change careers or a student looking to start one, this might be a good opportunity (even if they don't meet all the minimum requirements).

Davidson College is located in the Charlotte Metro area in North Carolina.
