The Research Computing Center at the University of Chicago is adding a Senior HPC System Administrator to our growing team. We are seeking a highly qualified, experienced individual to join the system and operation team that builds and manages RCC HPC systems and facility operations. The individual in this position will be involved in the procurement and management of HPC hardware and software.

More detailed information about the position and a link to apply can be found on the [University of Chicago Careers page](https://uchicago.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/External/job/Chicago-IL/Sr-HPC-System-Administrator_JR34672?q=rcc).
